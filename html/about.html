Location


Clinton Town

Clinton New Jersey is a relativly small town located in the upper middle section of Hunterdon County, which is itself located in western-middle Jersey. The town is surrounded by the Clinton, Franklin, and Union Townships. Its just about 1.5 square miles in size, with 0.1 of those square miles being water. Its most notible landmark is the Raritan River, the one that flows throught the famous red mill.

Clinton Township

Clinton Township is a seperate and significantly larger entity than the town. Its located right to the east of it, and is 33.87 square miles in size, with 4 of those being water. Its home to many different landmarks from Spruce Run lake to Bunt Park, many different neighborhoods being nested within it.
